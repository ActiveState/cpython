Upgrade the bundled pip in :mod:`ensurepip` from 23.0.1 to 24.0.post3, the
ActiveState security release of pip 24.0 (the last pip that supports Python
3.7).  Its vendored certifi CA bundle (2026.10.23) drops the emSign roots of
the compromised eMudhra/emSign CA except emSign Root CA - G1, and it fixes
CVE-2023-5752, CVE-2025-8869, CVE-2026-1703, CVE-2026-3219, CVE-2026-6357,
CVE-2026-8643 and CVE-2026-13346.
